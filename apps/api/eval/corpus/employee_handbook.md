# Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook

## Leave and Time Off

Full-time employees accrue **22 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly and unused days may be carried into the next year up to a
maximum of 8 days. Anything above that is forfeited on 31 January.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and is capped at 12 days per year. A
medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence.
Sick leave does not carry over.

Parental leave is 18 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for
the secondary caregiver. Requests are filed under policy **HR-204** and must
reach People Operations at least 60 days before the expected start date.

Unpaid sabbatical is available after four years of continuous service. The
maximum sabbatical length is 12 weeks and it is governed by policy **HR-311**.

## Expenses and Reimbursement

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to **$75 per day** in North
America and **$95 per day** elsewhere. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

Rail travel under six hours must be booked in standard class. Flights longer
than eight hours may be booked in premium economy with prior approval from a
director. All bookings go through the Travelport portal.

Home office equipment is reimbursed up to **$1,200** in the first year of
employment and **$400** per year afterwards. Monitors, chairs and desks are
covered; personal phones are not.

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims filed
after 45 days require written approval from the finance lead and are processed
under exception code **FIN-88**.

## Equipment and Assets

Every laptop carries an asset tag in the format LT-nnnn. The standard engineering
laptop is asset class **LT-4471**, a 16-inch machine with 32 GB of memory. The
design team uses asset class **LT-5290**, which ships with a colour-calibrated
display.

Loaner hardware is requested through the IT desk and must be returned within 10
working days. Lost equipment is charged at depreciated value under policy
**IT-119**.

Laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Employees may purchase their retired
device at 15% of its original price.

## Security

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for every internal system. Hardware keys
are issued to anyone with production access.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Password reuse across internal systems
is prohibited and is detected automatically by the identity provider.

Security incidents are reported to security@northwind.example within one hour of
discovery. The on-call security engineer is reachable at extension **4402**.

Contractors receive access for a maximum of 90 days per engagement, renewable
once by their sponsoring manager.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require approval from both the reporting manager and the department head, and
are reviewed every six months.

Anyone working from a country other than their employment country for more than
21 days in a year must notify People Operations in advance for tax reasons.
