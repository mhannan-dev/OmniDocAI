## What is Google?

**Google** is a multinational technology company best known for its **search engine** (Google Search), which is the most widely used search platform in the world. 

**Key things Google does:**
- **Search Engine** – helps you find information, websites, images, videos, and more
- **Advertising** – runs online ads (Google Ads)
- **Email** – Gmail
- **Cloud Storage** – Google Drive
- **Maps** – Google Maps
- **Video** – YouTube (owned by Google)
- **Mobile OS** – Android
- **AI/Products** – Google Assistant, Google Lens, Gemini (their AI chatbot)

**Founded:** 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin  
**Parent Company:** Alphabet Inc.

## What is DeepSeek?

**DeepSeek** is a **Chinese artificial intelligence company** that builds advanced AI models, including large language models (LLMs) similar to ChatGPT.

**Key things about DeepSeek:**
- **AI Chatbot** – DeepSeek has its own chatbot that can answer questions, write content, solve problems, and more
- **Open-Source Models** – They release some of their AI models publicly, allowing developers to use and modify them
- **Cost-Effective** – Known for building powerful AI models at a much lower cost than competitors
- **Competitor** – Competes with OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude), etc.
- **Founded:** 2023 (relatively new, but made a big impact quickly)
- **Based in:** Hangzhou, China

**Why is DeepSeek famous?**  
They surprised the tech world in early 2025 by releasing a model (DeepSeek-V3 and R1) that performed at par with top Western AI models but was built with far less cost and computing power. This caused a stir in the AI industry and even affected stock markets!
